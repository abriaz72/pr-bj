How the engine plays

Every hand is dealt from a real 8-deck shoe. The engine plays the player's hand automatically using basic strategy, and the dealer hits on soft 17, stands on hard 17 and above.

If the dealer's up-card is an Ace or a ten-value card, the dealer checks the hidden card for blackjack before play continues.

No splitting

A pair is not played as two hands. It's scored as its ordinary total and played through the same rules as any other hand.

No doubling

Where basic strategy would normally double the stake, the engine simply takes one card instead, at no extra cost.
